# Vision

Our vision is to democratize AI by enabling researchers—regardless of their technical expertise or academic background—to seamlessly harness the power of AI in their work. We aim to bridge the gap between domain knowledge and AI capabilities by providing intuitive, low-code solutions that empower researchers to build, customize, and deploy AI-driven workflows effortlessly. By removing technical barriers, we strive to accelerate scientific discovery, foster innovation, and make AI an accessible tool for all.

# Objectives

- Simplify AI Pipeline Development to allow researchers to design and implement AI pipelines with minimal coding effort.
- Develop an intuitive user interface that enables researchers to define AI workflows without requiring extensive programming knowledge.
- Create an automated tool for generating AI pipelines based on high-level specifications.
- Incorporate reusable pipeline templates that can adapt to different research domains.
- Lower the entry barrier for domain experts without AI expertise by providing tools that enable effortless creation of AI-powered workflows.
- Identify and model common and variable features of AI pipelines across different scientific disciplines.
- Support domain-specific customization while ensuring a flexible and generalizable pipeline architecture.
